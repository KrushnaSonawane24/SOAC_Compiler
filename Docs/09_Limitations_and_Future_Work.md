# Limitations and Future Work

## Limitations

- No training support (inference-only)
- Limited model architectures (grows over time)
- TensorRT depends on GPU availability
- Analytical cost models are estimations and must be validated with benchmarks

## Future work

- Additional backends (OpenVINO, WebGPU)
- Stronger analytical models and calibration
- IR visualization and debugging tools
- Distributed compilation

## Why SOAC matters

SOAC makes AI deployment behave like compilation: deterministic, traceable, and repeatable. It replaces guesswork with explicit passes, constraints, and verifiable outputs. That is the difference between “it runs” and “it ships safely.”
